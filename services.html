Playtheory (associated with Nosce House or Nosce Cafe in Alexandra, Johannesburg) is a community and gaming initiative focused on youth esports, local gaming tournaments (like EA FC events), and interactive play-based learning

The name combines the Latin root nosce meaning know from nosce te ipsum or know thyself with the concept that play is a core mechanism for growth, learning, and evolution Play | Learn | Evolve
